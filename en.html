What to Know About Vaping

Vaping may look different from smoking, but the aerosol you inhale is not simply water vapour and may contain nicotine or other substances.

  • 01

    Why You Want to Vape Again

    Nicotine creates dependence and can make you feel like using again more often.

  • 02

    Using More Often Than You Notice

    Vapes can be easy to reach for again and again, making it hard to notice how much you're using.

  • 03

    More Than Water Vapour

    The aerosol may contain nicotine, chemicals, or fine particles.

  • 04

    Long-term Effects

    Long-term effects are still being studied, but that doesn't mean vaping is safe.

Frequently asked questions

Is my call confidential?

Yes. What you share stays private. We don't share your information without your say-so.

Does it cost anything to call or text?

Calling and texting Quitline 1600 is free, even from a mobile with no credit.

Do I have to be ready to quit completely?

No. You can call to cut down, ask questions, or just find out more — there's no pressure to commit to anything today.

Can you help if I only vape, not smoke cigarettes?

Yes. Quitline 1600 supports people who vape or use nicotine products, not only cigarette smokers.

What if I've tried to quit before and started again?

That's a normal part of the process. We'll help you figure out what to try differently this time.
